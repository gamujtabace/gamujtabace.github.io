About

Building companies and cloud platforms

I am Ghulam Ahmad Mujtaba: a technology founder, a multi-cloud architect, and a leader who still works close to the engineering. I work with clients and teams around the world.

My story

My career began in 2015 as a Linux and network administrator, running servers, hosting platforms and round-the-clock operations. That grounding in how systems really behave still shapes every design I make.

I moved into DevOps and cloud engineering, then into enterprise cloud architecture, where I delivered large Azure and Oracle Cloud environments and later AWS. Over ten years I have led on-premises to cloud migrations, built hybrid networks, designed disaster recovery and automated infrastructure at scale.

Today I pair that experience with leadership. I am CEO and Founder of MRX Digital Coin and MRX Digital Technologies Ltd, and Global CEO of BrightWay Technologies Pvt Ltd. I also serve as Senior Vice President of the National Business Foundation Pakistan and MPA International Organization.
